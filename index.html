Research Interests

Causal Reasoning for Driving

Identifying scene elements and interactions that explain why an autonomous vehicle takes a particular action.

Critical Factor Identification

Distinguishing decision-relevant factors from background objects and contextual information in driving scenes.

Counterfactual Reasoning

Studying how changes to relevant scene factors affect driving decisions and causal attributions.

Vision-Language Models

Developing training and evaluation approaches for visual grounding, temporal reasoning, and causal understanding.

Autonomous Driving Causal Inference VLM / VLA Counterfactual Learning Multimodal Reasoning

Research Directions

Critical Factors for Driving Decisions

Investigating how models identify the visual evidence that is relevant to ego-vehicle behavior, including interacting vehicles, vulnerable road users, and road constraints.

Spatio-Temporal Causal Understanding

Exploring how scene changes, object interactions, and driving context relate to the evolution of vehicle decisions.

Counterfactual Consistency and Evaluation

Exploring evaluation methods that test whether causal explanations remain consistent when relevant scene factors are changed while other conditions are controlled.
